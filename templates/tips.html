General Health Tips

  • Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily to stay hydrated.
  • Get 7-8 hours of quality sleep every night.
  • Avoid skipping meals, especially breakfast.
  • Limit junk food and sugary drinks.

Eat Balanced Meals

  • Include fruits, vegetables, and whole grains in your daily diet.
  • Reduce salt and oil while cooking.
  • Eat at regular intervals instead of long gaps between meals.
  • Avoid eating late at night.

Exercise Regularly

  • Do at least 30 minutes of physical activity daily.
  • Maintain correct posture during exercises to protect your spine and joints.
  • Stay hydrated before, during, and after your workout.
  • Dance, yoga, walking, cycling — anything that feels fun will keep you motivated.

Manage Stress

  • Practice meditation or yoga to calm your mind.
  • Stretch for 5-10 minutes in the morning to improve flexibility.
  • Maintain good posture while sitting and using gadgets.
  • Take short breaks from your phone or social media; it helps your brain recharge.
